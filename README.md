## 📊 Automated reports

At **Elife** I lead social intelligence operations for a global spirits brand group and a fitness chain. One of the main reports used to be 100% manual. I redesigned and automated it, with more custom metrics, competitor comparisons and recommendations.

> **~70% less production time (about 50 hours saved per month)**, with a more complete and more analytical deliverable.

The demos below reproduce the design of those reports. **All data in them is fictional**: brands, numbers, profiles, posts and links were invented, and no client data is used.

| Demo | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Brands social listening report](https://juliapaleixo.github.io/social-listening-report-brands-demo/) · [code](https://github.com/juliapaleixo/social-listening-report-brands-demo) | Overview, one page per brand, competitor comparison, goals, daily evolution, top posts and insights |
| [Monthly social listening report](https://juliapaleixo.github.io/social-listening-report-monthly-demo/) · [code](https://github.com/juliapaleixo/social-listening-report-monthly-demo) | Daily volume, seasonality, peak-day exclusion, sentiment, audience, reasons, units and insights |

Also at Elife: an **AI agent built in n8n** that classifies about 90% of social mentions automatically and routes ambiguous cases to human review, freeing about 75 hours per month.
